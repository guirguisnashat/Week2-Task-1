About Lumen Tech Solutions

We are a team of engineers, designers and problem-solvers who believe technology should make work simpler, not harder.

Our story

From a two-person help desk to a trusted IT partner

Lumen started in 2014 when two engineers in Cairo noticed that small businesses were paying enterprise prices for slow, confusing IT support. They set out to offer something different: fast answers, plain language and honest pricing.

Today we are a team of 45 certified engineers, supporting more than 120 companies across healthcare, retail, logistics and finance in Egypt and the wider Middle East.

  • Microsoft, AWS and Cisco certified partners
  • ISO 27001 certified security practices
  • Offices in Cairo and Alexandria
